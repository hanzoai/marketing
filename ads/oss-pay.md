# Campaign — OSS Pay ("Your code earns 25% of the compute it powers")

**Hook:** "Your code earns 25% of the compute it powers."
**Audience:** OSS maintainers, model authors, research engineers.
**Phase:** 3 (OSS flywheel).
**Status honesty:** the author 25% compute-share is `[ROADMAP]` (GitLab +
per-compute attribution being completed). **All copy frames the model / the
program, not a delivered payout.** No maintainer testimonials or payout figures
until the rail ships and pays.

## Variant A — short
> Your code earns 25% of the compute it powers.
> Open source, finally paid — by the workload, not by charity.

## Variant B — social
> What if every time someone ran your open-source model on Hanzo, you got paid?
> Not a tip jar. Not GitHub Sponsors guilt. 25% of the actual compute your code
> powers, attributed per-run. That's the model we're building. Maintainers first.

## Variant C — the fairness argument (long)
> The internet runs on open source and pays its authors in stars.
> We think the compute is the honest unit: if your library, your model, your tool
> is what's doing the work, you should earn a share of what that work costs.
> Hanzo's plan: **25% of the compute your code powers, paid to you**, attributed
> per-run through GitLab + per-compute accounting. Your project brings its users;
> the platform pays you for the load they create. Sustainable open source, by
> construction.

## Variant D — recruit-maintainers
> Maintainers: we're building a way for your project to pay you — 25% of the
> compute it powers on Hanzo. Free Pro while you build. Bring your users; earn on
> the workload. Early maintainers shape the program.

*(Ties to the edu/OSS free-tier in `discounts.md`.)*

## Variant E — for research authors
> Ship a model. Get paid when it runs.
> 25% of the compute your weights power, attributed to you. Research that funds
> the next research.

## Creative concept (assets via hanzoai/studio)
- A `git commit` → a compute meter spinning → a 25% slice routing back to the
  author's wallet. The loop made literal.
- Split bar: 75% platform/operator, 25% author — clean, honest, obviously fair.
- A maintainer's repo card with a live "earned from compute" figure (mock, clearly
  illustrative until real).

## Channel note
This is a **Phase-3** campaign — do NOT run it before the attribution rail can
actually pay, or it's a broken promise to exactly the community we most need to
trust us. Until then: warm relationship-building with maintainers, framed as
"we're building this, help us shape it," never "we pay this today."
