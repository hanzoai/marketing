# Ad concept — First 1,000

- **Phase:** Phase 1 — Paid acquisition + launch promo
- **Objective / goal:** `purchase` (first paid month), with `signup`/`activate` upstream
- **Audience:** Hot — waitlist + site visitors + activated-not-paid; then warm lookalikes of paying users
- **Channel(s):** Meta retargeting first, Google Search on high-intent terms, then cold prospecting
- **Landing page:** `app.hanzo.ai` upgrade / pricing page with the promo applied

## The idea

A real, scarce, honest offer: the first 1,000 paying users get 90% off their
first month. No fake countdown — the cap is a hard 1,000 redemptions, and the
discount is exactly one month, disclosed at checkout. Scarcity that's true
converts; scarcity that's fake refunds.

## Hooks (headlines to test)

1. First 1,000 users: 90% off your first month.
2. Go Pro for $4.90 — first month, first 1,000 only.
3. The founding cohort gets 90% off. Then it's gone.

## Primary text / script

Hanzo's founding cohort is 1,000 users. Join it and your first month is 90% off —
Pro for $4.90, Max for $20, Team for $19.90 a seat. Month two renews at the normal
price, no surprises. One per org. When 1,000 is hit, the offer closes.

## Call to action

Claim your spot → (matches the pricing page's primary CTA)

## Claims (mandatory — honesty rule)

| Claim in the ad | Status | Source / assumption |
|---|---|---|
| 90% off the first month | product-fact | Promo spec, `discounts.md` |
| First 1,000 paying users only (hard cap) | product-fact | Redemption cap, `discounts.md` |
| Pro $4.90 / Max $20 / Team $19.90 seat (month 1) | product-fact | Discount-by-plan table, `discounts.md` |
| List prices Pro $49 / Max $200 / Team $199 | product-fact | Plans table, `discounts.md` |
| Month 2 renews at list price | product-fact (disclosed) | Eligibility, `discounts.md` |
| One redemption per org | product-fact (disclosed) | Abuse guard, `discounts.md` |

## Visual direction

A counter showing spots remaining out of 1,000 (driven by the **real** redemption
count, never a fake timer), and a clean before/after price. Produce in Studio.

## Do-not-say

- No fake countdown timers or invented "spots left" numbers — only the real
  redemption count.
- Do not imply the discount recurs; it is month 1 only.
- No unverified competitor price comparisons.
